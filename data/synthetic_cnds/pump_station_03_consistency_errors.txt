1.1 PURPOSE

This document describes the control philosophy for Pump Station PS-03, transferring
process water from Storage Tank TK-301 to Distribution Header DH-003.

SECTION 3: OPERATING MODES
================================================================================

3.1 STOPPED MODE

Entry conditions: Shutdown completed or ESD triggered
Actions: All pumps stopped, all valves in fail-safe positions

3.2 RUNNING MODE (Normal Operation)

Normal operating ranges:
  Discharge pressure: 65 to 130 PSI
  Process flow: 75 to 110 L/min
  Tank level: 20% to 85%
  Motor temperature: 15 to 75 degrees Celsius

3.3 EMERGENCY SHUTDOWN MODE

Entry conditions: Any ESD interlock triggers or operator presses ESD button
Actions: P-301 and P-302 de-energised immediately, all valves to fail-safe positions
Reset: Manual investigation and reset required before restart permitted

SECTION 6: INTERLOCKS
================================================================================

6.1 INTERLOCK IL-301 — HIGH-HIGH DISCHARGE PRESSURE TRIP

Cause:        PT-301 discharge pressure exceeds 150 PSI for more than 3 seconds
              [ERROR CON1: Prose says 150 PSI but setpoint table says 145 PSI for PT-301 HH Trip]
Action:       Stop pump P-301, close discharge valve XV-302
              Open alarm: "HIGH-HIGH DISCHARGE PRESSURE"
Reset Condition: PT-301 pressure falls below 120 PSI and cause investigated
Reset Method: Manual reset required
Priority:     Safety Critical

6.2 INTERLOCK IL-302 — LOW-LOW TANK LEVEL TRIP

Cause:        LT-501 tank level falls below 10%
Action:       Stop pump P-301 to prevent dry running
              Open alarm: "LOW-LOW TANK LEVEL"
Reset Condition: LT-501 level above 20% and tank supply restored
Reset Method: Manual reset required
Priority:     Safety Critical

6.3 INTERLOCK IL-303 — LOW-LOW FLOW TRIP

Cause:        FT-401 flow below 8 L/min for 10 seconds while P-301 running
Action:       Stop pump P-301 (discharge blockage or overheating risk)
              Open alarm: "NO FLOW DETECTED"
Reset Condition: FT-401 flow above 25 L/min and downstream clear
Reset Method: Manual reset required
Priority:     Safety Critical

6.4 INTERLOCK IL-304 — HIGH-HIGH MOTOR TEMPERATURE TRIP

Cause:        TT-601 motor temperature exceeds 90 degrees Celsius
Action:       Stop pump P-301 immediately
              Open alarm: "HIGH-HIGH MOTOR TEMPERATURE"
Reset Condition: TT-601 temperature below 70 degrees Celsius
Reset Method: Manual reset required
Priority:     Safety Critical
