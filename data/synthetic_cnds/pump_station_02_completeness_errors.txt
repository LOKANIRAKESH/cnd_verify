SECTION 1: GENERAL DESCRIPTION
================================================================================

1.1 PURPOSE

This document describes the control philosophy and operating requirements for Pump
Station PS-02, a water transfer system designed to move process water from Storage
Tank TK-201 to the Distribution Header DH-002.

1.2 SYSTEM DESCRIPTION

The pump station consists of two centrifugal pumps (P-201 as duty pump and P-202
as standby pump), associated valves, and instrumentation for pressure, flow, level,
and temperature monitoring. The system is controlled by a PLC-based BPCS.

1.3 DESIGN BASIS

Design flow rate: 120 litres per minute (L/min)
Design pressure: 180 PSI maximum
Operating pressure range: 70 to 140 PSI
Storage tank capacity: 40,000 litres
