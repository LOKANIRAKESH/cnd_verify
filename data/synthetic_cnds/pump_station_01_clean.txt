SECTION 1: GENERAL DESCRIPTION
================================================================================

1.1 PURPOSE

This document describes the control philosophy and operating requirements for Pump
Station PS-01, a water transfer system designed to move process water from Storage
Tank TK-101 to the Distribution Header DH-001. The pump station operates
continuously during plant operation and provides flow to downstream processes.

1.2 SYSTEM DESCRIPTION

The pump station consists of two centrifugal pumps (P-101 as duty pump and P-102
as standby pump), associated suction and discharge isolation valves, a flow control
valve, and instrumentation for pressure, flow, level, and temperature monitoring.
The system is controlled by a PLC-based Basic Process Control System (BPCS).

1.3 DESIGN BASIS

Design flow rate: 150 litres per minute (L/min)
Design pressure: 200 PSI maximum
Operating pressure range: 80 to 150 PSI
Storage tank capacity: 50,000 litres
Operating temperature range: 10 degrees Celsius to 60 degrees Celsius

================================================================================
SECTION 2: EQUIPMENT LIST
================================================================================

2.1 PUMPS

P-101 — Duty Pump
  Type: Centrifugal pump, horizontal, end-suction
  Rated flow: 150 L/min at 120 PSI
  Motor: 15 kW, 415V, 3-phase
  Driven by: Variable Frequency Drive VFD-101
  Normal state: Running during normal operation

P-102 — Standby Pump
  Type: Centrifugal pump, horizontal, end-suction (identical to P-101)
  Rated flow: 150 L/min at 120 PSI
  Motor: 15 kW, 415V, 3-phase
  Driven by: Direct Online Starter (DOL)
  Normal state: Stopped (standby, ready to start)

2.2 VALVES

XV-101 — Suction Isolation Valve (P-101)
  Type: Ball valve, pneumatically actuated, 6-inch NB
  Fail position: Fail-Open
  Normal state: Open during operation, Closed when P-101 is stopped
  Signal: 24VDC solenoid, energise-to-close

XV-102 — Suction Isolation Valve (P-102)
  Type: Ball valve, pneumatically actuated, 6-inch NB
  Fail position: Fail-Open
  Normal state: Closed when P-102 is in standby, Open when P-102 is running
  Signal: 24VDC solenoid, energise-to-close

XV-201 — Discharge Isolation Valve (P-101)
  Type: Ball valve, pneumatically actuated, 6-inch NB
  Fail position: Fail-Closed
  Normal state: Open when P-101 is running, Closed when P-101 is stopped
  Signal: 24VDC solenoid, energise-to-open

FV-301 — Flow Control Valve
  Type: Globe valve, pneumatically actuated, modulating, 4-inch NB
  Fail position: Fail-Closed
  Control range: 0% to 100% open
  Signal: 4-20 mA from FIC-201
  Purpose: Controls flow rate to Distribution Header DH-001

2.3 INSTRUMENTS

PT-101 — Discharge Pressure Transmitter
  Type: Electronic pressure transmitter
  Range: 0 to 250 PSI
  Output: 4-20 mA, HART
  Location: Pump P-101 discharge header, upstream of XV-201
  Purpose: Monitors discharge pressure, triggers high pressure trip

FT-201 — Process Flow Transmitter
  Type: Electromagnetic flow meter
  Range: 0 to 300 L/min
  Output: 4-20 mA, HART
  Location: Downstream of FV-301, on main process line
  Purpose: Measures flow to distribution header, provides feedback to FIC-201

LT-301 — Storage Tank Level Transmitter
  Type: Guided wave radar level transmitter
  Range: 0 to 100% (0 to 5 metres)
  Output: 4-20 mA, HART
  Location: Top of Storage Tank TK-101
  Purpose: Monitors tank level, triggers low level trip to protect pump from dry running

TT-401 — Pump Motor Temperature Transmitter
  Type: PT100 RTD temperature element with transmitter
  Range: 0 to 150 degrees Celsius
  Output: 4-20 mA
  Location: P-101 motor bearing housing
  Purpose: Monitors motor temperature, triggers high temperature trip to prevent motor damage

2.4 CONTROLLERS

FIC-201 — Flow Indicating Controller
  Type: PID control function in PLC
  Measured variable: FT-201 (process flow)
  Setpoint: 120 L/min (adjustable by operator, range 50 to 180 L/min)
  Output: 4-20 mA to FV-301
  Control action: Increase FV-301 opening when flow is below setpoint
  Mode: Auto mode during normal operation, Manual mode available to operator

SECTION 3: OPERATING MODES
================================================================================

3.1 STOPPED MODE

Description: All equipment is at rest. No pumping occurs.
Entry conditions:
  - Normal shutdown sequence completed, OR
  - Emergency shutdown triggered, OR
  - Startup sequence not yet initiated
Actions when entering STOPPED mode:
  - Pump P-101 motor stopped and de-energised
  - XV-101 closed (solenoid energised)
  - XV-201 closed (solenoid de-energised, fail-closed)
  - FV-301 closed (0% output from FIC-201)
Exit conditions:
  - Operator selects START from HMI and all startup permissives are satisfied

3.2 STARTING MODE (Startup Sequence)

Description: Transitional mode while pump station is being brought online.
Entry conditions: Operator initiates startup AND all permissives below are satisfied
  Permissive 1: LT-301 tank level greater than 30% (minimum to prevent pump dry running)
  Permissive 2: No active fault conditions on P-101 motor
  Permissive 3: No active interlock trips (all interlocks in reset state)
  Permissive 4: XV-101 in CLOSED position confirmed by limit switch
Exit conditions: Startup sequence completed and flow confirmed above 50 L/min

3.3 RUNNING MODE (Normal Operation)

Description: System is operating normally. P-101 is running and delivering flow to distribution header.
Entry conditions: Startup sequence completed successfully
Actions during RUNNING mode:
  - FIC-201 in AUTO mode, maintaining flow setpoint of 120 L/min
  - P-102 remains in hot standby (ready to start within 10 seconds)
  - All alarms enabled and active
Normal operating ranges:
  - Discharge pressure PT-101: 80 to 150 PSI
  - Process flow FT-201: 100 to 140 L/min (setpoint 120 L/min)
  - Tank level LT-301: 30% to 90%
  - Motor temperature TT-401: 20 to 75 degrees Celsius
Exit conditions: Shutdown initiated by operator, or interlock trip, or loss of supply

3.4 STANDBY MODE

Description: System is in hot standby. P-101 has stopped but P-102 is ready to take over.
Entry conditions: P-101 trips or fault, P-102 auto-start logic enables
Actions when entering STANDBY:
  - P-101 stopped and isolated
  - P-102 startup sequence initiated automatically
Exit conditions: P-102 running confirms and system returns to RUNNING mode

3.5 STOPPING MODE (Normal Shutdown)

Description: Controlled shutdown of pump station.
Entry conditions: Operator selects SHUTDOWN from HMI
Exit conditions: All equipment confirmed stopped and valves confirmed closed

3.6 EMERGENCY SHUTDOWN MODE (ESD)

Description: Immediate shutdown of all equipment due to unsafe condition.
Entry conditions: Any ESD interlock triggers, OR operator presses ESD pushbutton on panel
Actions on ESD activation (all happen simultaneously):
  - P-101 motor de-energised immediately
  - P-102 motor de-energised immediately
  - XV-101 opens (fail-open — suction protected)
  - XV-201 closes (fail-closed — discharge isolated)
  - FV-301 closes (fail-closed — flow stopped)
  - ESD alarm annunciated at HMI with cause displayed
  - ESD latch activated — system cannot restart until manually reset
Reset procedure:
  - Investigate and resolve the ESD cause
  - Confirm all process conditions are within safe limits
  - Operator acknowledges ESD alarm on HMI
  - Operator resets ESD latch from local panel reset button
  - System returns to STOPPED mode after successful reset
Exit conditions: ESD latch manually reset by operator after cause resolved

================================================================================
SECTION 4: CONTROL LOOPS
================================================================================

4.1 FLOW CONTROL LOOP — FIC-201

Objective: Maintain flow to Distribution Header DH-001 at the operator-specified setpoint.
Sensor: FT-201 (electromagnetic flow meter, downstream of FV-301)
Controller: FIC-201 (PID, in PLC)
Final element: FV-301 (globe valve, 4-inch, modulating)
Setpoint: 120 L/min (default), operator adjustable 50 to 180 L/min
PID parameters: To be tuned during commissioning. Initial values: P=1.5, I=30 seconds, D=0
Control action: Direct acting — flow below setpoint causes FV-301 to open further

4.2 PRESSURE MONITORING — PT-101

Note: PT-101 is a monitoring instrument only. There is no automatic pressure control.
The pressure in the system is determined by the pump characteristics and FV-301 position.
PT-101 is used for alarm and interlock functions only (see Sections 6 and 7).

SECTION 6: INTERLOCKS
================================================================================

6.1 INTERLOCK IL-001 — HIGH-HIGH DISCHARGE PRESSURE TRIP

Cause:        PT-101 discharge pressure exceeds 150 PSI for more than 3 seconds
Action:       Stop pump P-101 (de-energise motor)
              Close discharge valve XV-201
              Open alarm on HMI: "HIGH-HIGH DISCHARGE PRESSURE — PT-101"
Reset Condition: PT-101 pressure falls below 130 PSI AND operator has investigated cause
Reset Method: Manual reset required — operator must press RESET button on HMI
Priority:     Safety Critical

6.2 INTERLOCK IL-002 — LOW-LOW TANK LEVEL TRIP

Cause:        LT-301 storage tank level falls below 20%
Action:       Stop pump P-101 immediately to prevent pump running dry
              Close discharge valve XV-201
              Open alarm on HMI: "LOW-LOW TANK LEVEL — LT-301 — PUMP PROTECTION"
Reset Condition: LT-301 level rises above 30% (tank refilled) AND operator confirms tank level adequate
Reset Method: Manual reset required — operator must press RESET button on HMI
Priority:     Safety Critical

6.3 INTERLOCK IL-003 — LOW-LOW FLOW (NO-FLOW) TRIP

Cause:        FT-201 flow rate falls below 10 L/min for more than 10 seconds while P-101 is running
Action:       Stop pump P-101 (indicates blocked discharge or closed valve — overheating risk)
              Open alarm on HMI: "NO-FLOW DETECTED — FT-201 — PUMP TRIP"
Reset Condition: FT-201 flow reading above 30 L/min AND all downstream valves confirmed open
Reset Method: Manual reset required — operator must investigate blocked discharge before restart
Priority:     Safety Critical

6.4 INTERLOCK IL-004 — HIGH-HIGH MOTOR TEMPERATURE TRIP

Cause:        TT-401 motor temperature exceeds 90 degrees Celsius
Action:       Stop pump P-101 immediately
              Open alarm on HMI: "HIGH-HIGH MOTOR TEMPERATURE — TT-401 — MOTOR PROTECTION"
Reset Condition: TT-401 motor temperature falls below 70 degrees Celsius AND motor cooling fan confirmed running
Reset Method: Manual reset required — operator must confirm motor has cooled sufficiently
Priority:     Safety Critical

6.5 INTERLOCK IL-005 — MOTOR FAULT TRIP

Cause:        Motor protection relay on P-101 detects fault (overload, phase loss, earth fault)
Action:       Stop pump P-101 immediately (motor protection relay also trips independently)
              Open alarm on HMI: "P-101 MOTOR FAULT — CHECK MOTOR PROTECTION RELAY"
Reset Condition: Motor protection relay manually reset at MCC, fault cause identified and rectified
Reset Method: Manual reset at MCC AND manual reset on HMI required
Priority:     Safety Critical

SECTION 7: ALARMS
================================================================================

7.1 PT-101 — HIGH DISCHARGE PRESSURE ALARM

Tag:              PT-101-H
Alarm Type:       High (H)
Setpoint:         130 PSI
Units:            PSI
Priority:         Priority 2 (HIGH)
Operator Response: Investigate cause of elevated pressure. Check if FV-301 is partially closed or
                   downstream valve is restricting flow. Reduce FV-301 setpoint if required.
                   If pressure continues to rise toward 150 PSI trip, prepare for manual shutdown.
Probable Cause:   Downstream flow restriction, FV-301 control issue, pressure surge

7.2 PT-101 — HIGH-HIGH DISCHARGE PRESSURE ALARM (Pre-Trip Warning)

Tag:              PT-101-HH
Alarm Type:       High High (HH)
Setpoint:         150 PSI
Units:            PSI
Priority:         Priority 1 (CRITICAL)
Operator Response: IMMEDIATE ACTION REQUIRED. Interlock IL-001 will trip pump automatically.
                   Acknowledge alarm and confirm pump has stopped. Investigate cause before attempting restart.
Probable Cause:   Complete flow blockage downstream, valve failure, pipe obstruction

7.3 PT-101 — LOW DISCHARGE PRESSURE ALARM

Tag:              PT-101-L
Alarm Type:       Low (L)
Setpoint:         60 PSI
Units:            PSI
Priority:         Priority 3 (MEDIUM)
Operator Response: Check pump P-101 operation. Verify VFD-101 is running at expected speed.
                   Check suction valve XV-101 is fully open. Check for any leaks in discharge pipework.
Probable Cause:   Pump wear, VFD speed below setpoint, suction valve partially closed

7.4 FT-201 — LOW FLOW ALARM

Tag:              FT-201-L
Alarm Type:       Low (L)
Setpoint:         20 L/min
Units:            L/min
Priority:         Priority 2 (HIGH)
Operator Response: Check FV-301 is responding to FIC-201 output. Check for partial blockage in flow line.
                   Check FT-201 reading is accurate (possible meter fouling). Increase FIC-201 setpoint if
                   downstream demand has increased.
Probable Cause:   FV-301 not fully open, partial downstream blockage, increased downstream demand

7.5 LT-301 — LOW TANK LEVEL ALARM

Tag:              LT-301-L
Alarm Type:       Low (L)
Setpoint:         30 %
Units:            %
Priority:         Priority 2 (HIGH)
Operator Response: Contact raw water supply team to arrange tank refill. Reduce pump flow rate
                   to conserve remaining water. Monitor closely — Low Low trip at 20% will stop pump.
Probable Cause:   Insufficient tank refill rate, higher than normal consumption, inlet valve closed

7.6 LT-301 — HIGH TANK LEVEL ALARM

Tag:              LT-301-H
Alarm Type:       High (H)
Setpoint:         90 %
Units:            %
Priority:         Priority 3 (MEDIUM)
Operator Response: Contact raw water supply team to reduce inlet flow to tank.
                   Monitor — if level continues to rise, manually close inlet valve.
Probable Cause:   Inlet valve open too far, higher than expected refill rate

7.7 TT-401 — HIGH MOTOR TEMPERATURE ALARM

Tag:              TT-401-H
Alarm Type:       High (H)
Setpoint:         75 degrees Celsius
Units:            Celsius
Priority:         Priority 2 (HIGH)
Operator Response: Check motor cooling fan is running. Check ambient temperature around motor.
                   Reduce pump load if possible. If temperature continues to rise toward 90 degrees C trip,
                   prepare for controlled shutdown.
Probable Cause:   Motor cooling fan failure, high ambient temperature, motor overload

SECTION 8: STARTUP SEQUENCE
================================================================================

Pre-startup checks (all must be confirmed before startup is initiated):
  CHECK 1: Confirm tank level LT-301 is above 30%
  CHECK 2: Confirm no active fault alarms on P-101 motor or VFD-101
  CHECK 3: Confirm XV-101 is in CLOSED position (limit switch confirms)
  CHECK 4: Confirm XV-201 is in CLOSED position (limit switch confirms)
  CHECK 5: Confirm FV-301 is at 0% (closed)
  CHECK 6: Confirm P-102 standby pump is available

Startup sequence:
  Step 1: Operator selects PUMP STATION START from HMI main screen
  Step 2: PLC checks all permissives listed above — if any fail, startup is aborted with fault message
  Step 3: Open suction valve XV-101 to fully open
          Confirmation: XV-101 open limit switch confirms within 15 seconds, else abort
  Step 4: Start pump P-101 via VFD-101 at minimum speed (30 Hz)
          Confirmation: P-101 motor current reading within normal range within 10 seconds, else abort
  Step 5: Slowly ramp VFD-101 speed from 30 Hz to 50 Hz over 30 seconds
  Step 6: Open discharge valve XV-201 to fully open
          Confirmation: XV-201 open limit switch confirms within 15 seconds, else abort
  Step 7: Enable FIC-201 in AUTO mode with setpoint of 120 L/min
          FV-301 will begin opening to establish flow
  Step 8: Confirm flow FT-201 reads above 50 L/min within 60 seconds
          If flow not established within 60 seconds, abort startup and raise alarm
  Step 9: Startup complete — system transitions to RUNNING mode
          HMI displays: "PUMP STATION PS-01 — RUNNING"

================================================================================
SECTION 9: SHUTDOWN SEQUENCE
================================================================================

Normal shutdown sequence:
  Step 1: Operator selects PUMP STATION STOP from HMI main screen
  Step 2: FIC-201 transfers to MANUAL mode, ramp FV-301 to 0% over 30 seconds
  Step 3: Confirm flow FT-201 drops below 20 L/min
  Step 4: Stop pump P-101 via VFD-101 (ramp down over 15 seconds)
  Step 5: Confirm P-101 motor current returns to zero
  Step 6: Close discharge valve XV-201
          Confirmation: XV-201 closed limit switch confirms within 15 seconds
  Step 7: Close suction valve XV-101
          Confirmation: XV-101 closed limit switch confirms within 15 seconds
  Step 8: System transitions to STOPPED mode
          HMI displays: "PUMP STATION PS-01 — STOPPED"
